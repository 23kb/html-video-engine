Our Bulb Planting Guide for Big Color Next Year

by Rosa Reyes | Sep 13, 2026 | Seasonal Guides | 0 comments

Bulbs are the easiest way to get early color. Plant them once, and they come back for years.

Plant at the right depth

A simple rule: plant each bulb three times as deep as it is tall, pointy end up. Space tulips about four inches apart.

Choose a spot with good drainage. Bulbs rot in wet soil. Mix in early native bloomers so bees find food early.
