Composting 101: Turn Kitchen Scraps into Garden Gold

by Rosa Reyes | Aug 20, 2026 | Garden Care | 0 comments

Composting turns kitchen scraps and yard waste into dark, crumbly soil food. It is free, and it keeps waste out of the landfill.

What goes in

Mix "greens" such as vegetable peels, coffee grounds and fresh grass with "browns" such as dry leaves, straw and plain cardboard. Aim for about three parts brown to one part green.

Keep meat, dairy and oily food out of the pile. They smell and attract pests.

Keep it working

Turn the pile every week or two and keep it as damp as a wrung-out sponge. A good watering schedule for the garden works for the compost bin too.

Finished compost goes on top of every vegetable bed each year. For a local soil test, ask your county extension office.
