How to Plan a Vegetable Bed That Feeds You All Year

by Rosa Reyes | Aug 9, 2026 | Vegetables | 0 comments

A good vegetable bed starts on paper. Before you dig, decide what your family eats most, how much sun the bed gets, and how much time you have each week.

Pick the right spot

Most vegetables need six to eight hours of direct sun. Watch the spot for a full day and note when shade from trees or fences arrives.

Good soil matters more than a big space. Mix in two inches of finished compost before you plant. Our Composting 101 guide shows how to make your own.

Plan in blocks, not rows

Plant in square blocks so you can reach every plant without stepping on the soil. If you are short on space, raised beds warm up faster and drain better.

If you have no yard at all, container herbs are an easy first crop on a sunny step.

Keep a simple notebook. Write down what you planted, when, and how it did. Next year you will plan faster and waste less seed.
