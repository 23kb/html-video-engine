Process

Four phases

Each phase ends with a review and a sign-off, so nothing moves on until you are happy.

1

Discover

Two workshops with your team, interviews with five customers and a brief everyone signs.
2

Define

Positioning, naming and the creative route, presented as two directions.
3

Design

Identity, site and print, built in weekly reviews on a shared board.
4

Deliver

Final files, brand guidelines and a launch plan your team can run.
Deliverables

What each phase hands over

DiscoverWeeks 1–2
DefineWeeks 3–4
DesignWeeks 5–8
DeliverWeeks 9–10
DiscoverWeeks 1–2

A research summary, five customer interviews, a competitor map and a one-page brief. You sign the brief before we draw a single line.

DefineWeeks 3–4

Positioning statement, naming shortlist with trademark pre-checks, and two creative routes presented in person or on a call.

DesignWeeks 5–8

Logo suite, colour and type system, key layouts and the website design system, reviewed every Thursday.

DeliverWeeks 9–10

Final artwork in every format, a 40-page brand guide, a launch checklist and 30 days of support after go-live.

Engagements

Project or retainer?

Project
Retainer

A fixed-scope project

One brief, one price, one launch date. Best for a new brand, a rebrand or a new website. Most projects run eight to twelve weeks.

A monthly retainer

A set number of studio days each month for campaigns, new pages and print. Best for teams that ship every week. Minimum three months.

Our approach

Why we keep the team small

Senior people on every project

Every Northline project is run by the people you meet in the first call. We do not hand work to juniors after the pitch, and we do not subcontract design.

That is also why we cap the studio at eight projects a year. It keeps reviews short, decisions fast and the work consistent from the first sketch to the last file.

When a project needs a specialist we do not have, such as an illustrator or a photographer, we bring in people we have worked with for years and introduce them on day one.
