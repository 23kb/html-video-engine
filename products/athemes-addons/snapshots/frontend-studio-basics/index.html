Brand, web and print for teams who ship

Northline Studio is a six-person design studio in Portland. We build identities, websites and packaging for founders, publishers and makers, and we take on eight projects a year.

Start a project

What we do

Four disciplines, one team. Every project is run by the people you meet in the first call.

Brand Identity

Names, marks and systems that last, with guidelines your team will actually use.

Web Design

WordPress sites with a design system, built to load fast and easy to edit.

Print & Editorial

Books, reports and signage, managed through to the press check.

Our process

Discover, define, design and deliver. Each phase ends with a review and a sign-off, so nothing moves on until you are happy. Most projects run eight to twelve weeks from the first workshop to launch day, with a review every Thursday and one shared board for files, notes and decisions.
