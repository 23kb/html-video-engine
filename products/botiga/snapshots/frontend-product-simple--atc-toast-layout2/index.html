Description

The Velvet Swivel Chair turns toward the conversation, the fire or the window. The seat sits on a hidden steel swivel, so the chair looks like a soft sculpture from every side.

The velvet is woven to resist pilling and cleans with water.

Materials and care

  • Solid hardwood frame, FSC certified
  • Water-based, low-VOC finish
  • Dust with a dry cloth; avoid direct heat
  • 5-year Fernhill frame warranty
