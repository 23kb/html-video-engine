Description

Bent oak plywood forms the wings, and a cushioned leather seat floats between them. The Curved Lounge Chair is low, wide and made for leaning back.

The plywood is pressed in our Portland workshop from seven layers of oak veneer.

Materials and care

  • Solid hardwood frame, FSC certified
  • Water-based, low-VOC finish
  • Dust with a dry cloth; avoid direct heat
  • 5-year Fernhill frame warranty
