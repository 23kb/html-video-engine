Homemade Granola Recipe (Our Pantry Favorite)

This is the granola we make every Sunday at Archie’s Pantry. It keeps for two weeks in a jar and takes about 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup chopped almonds
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp cinnamon and a pinch of sea salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F (160°C).
  2. Mix everything in a big bowl until the oats are coated.
  3. Spread on a lined tray and bake for 25 minutes, stirring once.
  4. Let it cool on the tray so it forms clusters.
