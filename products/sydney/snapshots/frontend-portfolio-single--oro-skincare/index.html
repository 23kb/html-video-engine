Oro Skincare

Golden packaging for a clean-beauty line.

Client: Oro Skincare  ·  Services: Packaging  ·  Year: 2024

The brief

Oro makes small-batch skincare with Oregon botanicals. We designed a calm serif identity and a jar-and-carton system that reads at a glance on a crowded counter and keeps the ingredient list honest and readable.

The result

Oro launched in 60 boutiques, and the carton won a Dieline award for sustainable packaging.
