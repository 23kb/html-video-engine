Why we start every project with a workshop

Every Northline project begins the same way: three hours, one room, your team and ours. No laptops, lots of sticky notes. It is the most valuable half-day of the whole engagement.

What happens in the room

  • We agree on who the work is for, in one sentence.
  • We list what must be true when the project is done.
  • We name the three competitors you are most tired of being compared to.

By lunch we have a short brief that everyone has signed. It saves weeks of revisions later, because when a design is debated we can point back to the page and ask: does this do what we agreed?

The workshop was the moment our founders stopped arguing about colours and started talking about customers.

Daniel Ferro, Fernwood Coffee
