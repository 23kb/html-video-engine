About Northline

We are an independent design studio in Portland, Oregon. We build brands and websites for people who care how things are made.

Good design is clear first and clever second.

Every project starts with one question: what does this need to say, and to whom? We answer it before we draw a single shape. The result is work that reads fast and lasts for years.

Who we are

We are designers, writers and developers who like small teams and short feedback loops. You talk to the people doing the work, not an account manager.

Our clients range from family farms to venture-backed startups. What they share is a need to look as good as the thing they make.
