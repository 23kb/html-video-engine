Welcome to your WordPress Dashboard!

The Dashboard is the first place you will come to every time you log into your site. It is where you will find all your WordPress tools. If you need help, just click the “Help” tab above the screen title.

The left-hand navigation menu provides links to all of the WordPress administration screens, with submenu items displayed on hover. You can minimize this menu to a narrow icon strip by clicking on the Collapse Menu arrow at the bottom.

Links in the Toolbar at the top of the screen connect your dashboard and the front end of your site, and provide access to your profile and helpful WordPress information.

You can use the following controls to arrange your Dashboard screen to suit your workflow. This is true on most other administration screens as well.

Screen Options — Use the Screen Options tab to choose which Dashboard boxes to show.

Drag and Drop — To rearrange the boxes, drag and drop by clicking on the title bar of the selected box and releasing when you see a gray dotted-line rectangle appear in the location you want to place the box.

Box Controls — Click the title bar of the box to expand or collapse it. Some boxes added by plugins may have configurable content, and will show a “Configure” link in the title bar if you hover over it.

The boxes on your Dashboard screen are:

Welcome — Shows links for some of the most common tasks when setting up a new site.

Site Health Status — Informs you of any potential issues that should be addressed to improve the performance or security of your website.

At a Glance — Displays a summary of the content on your site and identifies which theme and version of WordPress you are using.

Activity — Shows the upcoming scheduled posts, recently published posts, and the most recent comments on your posts and allows you to moderate them.

Quick Draft — Allows you to create a new post and save it as a draft. Also displays links to the 3 most recent draft posts you've started.

WordPress Events and News — Upcoming events near you as well as the latest news from the official WordPress project and the WordPress Planet.

Screen elements

Some screen elements can be shown or hidden by using the checkboxes. Expand or collapse the elements by clicking on their headings, and arrange them by dragging their headings or by clicking on the up and down arrows.

WPForms Latest [2.0.3] - 2026-XX-XX

[2.0.3] - 2026-XX-XX

Added

  • Support for the Nigerian Naira (NGN), Ghanaian Cedi (GHS), and Kenyan Shilling (KES) currencies.
  • New Add Other Choice option for the Checkboxes field lets users type their own answer when none of the predefined choices fit.
  • The Coupon field now has a Coupons Discount setting in the Form Builder to choose whether a coupon discounts the whole order, everything except selected payment fields, or only selected payment fields.
  • The Form Builder now tells site owners that PDFs are generated on WPForms servers in the United States before the PDF addon is installed or activated.
  • Prefill by URL now preselects Checkbox Items, Multiple Items, and Dropdown Items choices when the amount in the link is written in the site currency's format, such as 10,50 or 1,200.00.
  • The Form Builder now spells the PayPal Commerce addon name correctly in payment settings and addon install prompts.
  • WPForms now logs a notification that is not sent because its "Send To" has no valid email address, even when logging is disabled, and logs invalid addresses removed from "Send To" or CC.
  • Keyboard shortcuts and the Move Up and Move Down buttons in the Form Builder can now move a field into and out of Layout and Repeater fields.

Changed

  • Improved how the Stripe connection data is stored.
  • Updated DOMPurify library to 3.4.15.
  • Forms using the older anti-spam honeypot or token check are now automatically upgraded to modern anti-spam protection, with each form's previous version saved as a revision you can restore.
  • Failures to start background processes are now recorded if WPForms Logs is enabled.
  • Updated the SVG sanitization library for improved security and compatibility with files from older graphic editors.
  • Clarified the anti-spam settings in the Form Builder: the modern protection toggle is now labeled "Enable anti-spam protection", the older setting is labeled as legacy, and the note on auto-upgraded forms explains how to switch back.
  • The confirmation message now receives focus after an AJAX form submission, so screen readers announce it to visitors.
  • Screen readers now announce the actual validation message instead of "Error message" for invalid fields when Modern Markup is enabled.
  • Columns on the Forms Overview page were reordered, and the Tags and Author columns are now hidden by default.
  • The weekly Email Summary now shows key stats with week-over-week trends, a breakdown of entries by form, and highlights tailored to the site's activity and setup.

Fixed

  • The Export button on Tools > Export stayed enabled after every form was deselected on WordPress 5.5.
  • Checkbox and Multiple Choice icons were missing on the entry Print Preview page, and check marks overlapped choice text.
  • The Camera field did not fully follow the form's style settings: the selected file name used a fixed gray color, and the capture button took its border radius and size from the Button settings instead of the Field settings.
  • Clicking a field in the Form Builder could open a different field's settings in forms built with WPForms AI. Saving then merged the two fields into one and moved the survivor up the form.
  • In the Form Builder, toggle options of a Map field responded to clicks and opened a warning popup while the field was disabled with the "Setup Required" label.
  • Undo and Redo in the Form Builder did not restore the Show/Hide action at the top of a Conditional Logic block in a field's Smart Logic settings, Notifications, and Confirmations.
  • Removed provider connection or settings block could not be redone in the Form Builder.
  • Fields placed inside Layout and Repeater columns were moved out of their container in the Form Builder preview after applying AI Form Edit changes.
  • The Visual tab of a Content field went blank and a browser console error appeared when switching between the Text and Visual tabs after a WPForms AI editing prompt reordered the form's fields.
  • Marking entries as Not Spam through the Bulk Actions dropdown in the Entries list only changed their status and skipped the notification email and other post-submission actions that the row-level Not Spam link performed.
  • A fatal error could occur on entries-related screens.
  • Stripe Custom Meta kept the empty Meta Key field highlighted after undoing the Object Type and Meta Value selections in the Form Builder.
  • A smart tag with a long label overflowed its field in the Form Builder and could not be fully reached with the keyboard; it is now truncated with the full label shown on hover.
  • A browser console error was thrown when conditional logic re-applied a default value to a field with an input mask, such as the Address field's ZIP code.
  • Fields with validation errors were not marked with the aria-invalid attribute for screen readers after form submission, only after leaving an individual field.
  • The standard Submit button appeared alongside the PayPal Commerce smart payment buttons in the Form Builder preview after undoing and redoing an AI Smart Form Editor change that removed the PayPal Commerce field.
  • PayPal Payments remained switched off and displayed a notice to enable it again after undoing the removal of a PayPal Commerce field.
  • Removing a PayPal Commerce field added three steps to the Form Builder undo history instead of one, causing two subsequent Undo presses to do nothing.
  • The Capture With Your Camera button of a Camera field placed in a 3-column Layout field overflowed its column on phones in landscape orientation.
  • Payment notices appeared in the Form Builder while the AI Smart Form Editor was applying changes, including right after it deleted the payment field the notice referred to.
  • The Setup Wizard handed off to its hosted version even when that version could not connect back to the site, so it failed on its first request instead of falling back to the local Getting Started setup.
  • Email addresses used as payment titles were displayed capitalized in the Payments table.
  • In the Form Builder, pressing Undo after removing a tag from a multi-select option, such as Allowed Coupons, cleared the whole selection instead of restoring the removed tag when that removal was the first change made to the option.
  • Constant Contact (V3): mapping an individual Address field subfield to the Street Address contact field silently dropped the address instead of sending the selected subfield value.
  • Submitting a form with a Dropdown Items or Multiple Items payment field failed with a fatal error instead of showing the "Invalid payment option" message when the field received a malformed value.
  • PayPal Commerce forms now always require a completed payment before saving an entry.
  • The forms and payments tables on the Dashboard page were missing space below their last row in Firefox.
  • Icon Choices did not follow the button background color in forms using Modern Markup on WordPress 5.5.
  • The page shifted horizontally when the What's New modal was opened or closed on WordPress 5.5.
  • Smart Tag values used inside embedded content in a in a Confirmation message or a form description were not always escaped properly.
  • Payment field prices could be displayed incorrectly when the site currency was switched to another currency.
