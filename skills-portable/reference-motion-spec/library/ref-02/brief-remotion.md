## Rules that made replicas work

1. **Build an exact replica.** Say those words in the build brief. Left alone, a builder adds re-aims, glides and settles the reference never had. Every landing and seam below is the whole movement budget.
2. **Read the seam strips before writing any transition.** A 4 fps read turns every 1–7-frame dissolve into a hard cut and the film ships blinks. The seam kinds below came from 24 fps strips; keep their frame counts.
3. **Camera, object and content motion are three different things.** A card growing is an object move; text typing inside a stable box is content; only a whole-composition reframe is camera. Build each with its own mechanism.
4. **A payoff arrives after a beat.** Show the "before" for the `before_hold` given, then the change with its own motion.
5. **Hold what the reference holds.** A hold is carried by the subject's own motion (typing, cursor, settle), not by a drift the reference does not have.

## Do not

- Exact replica: do not add camera glides, re-aims, pushes or settles the reference never had. The lens moves twice (9.75 settle, 11.04 pull); every other framing change is a 1-frame cut or a soft resolve in place.
- Do not crossfade any seam. No join here is a crossfade or a dip; softness is on the incoming side only, and the outgoing gets at most a 1-2-frame blur pulse.
- Do not build the card growth, tilt, drift or the chips' convergence as camera zoom - they are object motion on a fixed lens.
- Do not let headline words arrive white; they land in the accent green and cool to white ~0.35 s later.
- Do not fade the end-card lockup up from transparent - it is on the photo from the cut's first frame in dark teal and ramps to white; do not zoom the photo (it is static).
- Do not add a cursor approach: the pointer is already parked on the button when the card resolves and never moves; no cursor exists after 2.6.
- Do not replace the glitch swaps with a plain crossfade or slide: they are symbol scrambles re-rolling every ~2 frames with a grey block covering a word for 1-3 frames; the second swap is longer (0.83 s) than the first (0.46 s).
- Do not carry the 29.97 -> 24 fps jitter (repeated/dropped frames) into a build; timings here are +-1/24 s.
- Do not copy the reference's copy, logo, product UI or photo - technique only.
