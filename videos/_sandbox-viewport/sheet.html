Capture viewport — the candidates, on camera

Same live pages (sullies-bakery), five viewport sizes, shot 2026-08-22. Nothing here touched a real snapshot — scratch shots only, in videos/_sandbox-viewport/shots/. All images are shown at a common physical scale inside each section, so a bigger picture really is a bigger capture.

1 · The mechanics the numbers proved

The builder is a 100vh app — its document height equals the viewport height exactly (measured: 668 / 720 / 900 / 1080). The capture height IS the builder's entire world; everything else is pane-scrolling. Ordinary admin pages (settings: 1127px) and frontend pages (~2008px) keep their full height in the frozen DOM regardless of viewport — for them, height only decides what was "above the fold" at capture time.

6 · Weight

A non-issue. Builder shots: 72–99KB across all five sizes (1920×1080 costs +24% over legacy). The heavy pages are imagery (frontend contact: 650–854KB) and that cost is content, not viewport. Full-page frontend at 1280 wide: 826KB vs 650KB screenful.

7 · The decisions (my recommendations marked)

  1. Width → keep 1440. REC It's what the newest films already mount at capture parity (ranking-field: five stations at 1440×900, full-frame = 4/3), it's the code default, and at-rest text reads 17.3px on camera vs 13px for a 1920 capture. 1920 buys nothing at zoom (section 5) — only more horizontal pan range, at an at-rest legibility cost.
  2. Height → 1080. The headline change. REC New standard 1440×1080: the builder gets a 1080px workspace (+62% over legacy 668), portrait shorts get zero dead bands at the floor (section 4), landscape films get +360px of pan headroom (section 3). This removes the geometric CAUSE of the dead-band problem for every future builder-sourced short. Note: 1440×1080 is 4:3 — the camera crops, films never show the raster edge, so the aspect is invisible on camera.
  3. Screenful capture stays (no full-page mode). REC Scrolling pages keep their full DOM height in the frozen snapshot anyway — height only gated the 100vh builder and lazy-loaded content. Full-page adds weight and solves nothing the 1080 height doesn't.
  4. Migration → forward-only. REC Existing snapshots untouched (their films are tuned to them; rulebook: mount at capture parity). A page gets the new geometry when a video next needs a fresh capture of it. No bulk re-capture.
  5. Coordination fix found on the way: .env still carries WP_URL=http://newsite.local (dead host, 502) + stale WP_USER/WP_PASS. Any tool honoring the env override aims at a dead site — the exact rulebook rf 3c trap. Recommend blanking those three lines; tools/sites.json is the live registry.
